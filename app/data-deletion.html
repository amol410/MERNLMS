Account Deletion Request

⚠️

This action is permanent and cannot be undone

Once your account is deleted, all your data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days and cannot be recovered.

🗑️ What will be deleted

  • Your account (email, name, password)
  • Profile information and photo
  • All quiz attempt history and scores
  • Flashcard study progress and mastery data
  • Authentication sessions and tokens

📚 What will NOT be deleted

  • Shared course content (notes, videos, quizzes) — these are not yours to delete
  • Anonymised, aggregated statistics (if any)
⏱️   We will process your deletion request within 30 days of receiving your email.

How to submit your request

1
Click the button below — it will open your email app with a pre-filled deletion request.
2
Include your registered email address in the message so we can identify your account.
3
We will send you a confirmation email within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days.
